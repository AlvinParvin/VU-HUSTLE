Safety & Terms

Stay safe on campus

  • Meet in public places on campus (library, cafeteria, open areas).
  • Prefer daytime meetings.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting.
  • Do not share personal information beyond what is needed for the deal.
  • If something feels wrong, walk away and report the seller.

For buyers

  • Check the seller’s reviews and photos before paying.
  • Agree on price and meeting point clearly (preferably on WhatsApp).
  • Inspect the product before handing over money.

For sellers

  • Use real photos of your actual products.
  • Keep prices fixed and clear.
  • Respond to messages promptly and politely.
  • Do not harass or pressure buyers.

Terms of use

VU Hustle is a student directory. We do not handle payments or guarantee transactions. Listings are approved by admins but responsibility for the sale stays with the buyer and seller.

We may hide or remove listings and reviews that break these rules. False reports may result in your account being restricted.

By using this site you agree to these guidelines.
