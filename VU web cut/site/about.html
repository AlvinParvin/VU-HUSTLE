About VU Hustle

VU Hustle is a student-built directory that helps Victoria University students find and support campus businesses — from perfume and cakes to phone accessories and printing services.

Every listing is reviewed by an admin before it appears, so buyers see real photos, fixed prices in UGX, and honest reviews.

Our goals

  • Make it easy for student sellers to reach buyers on campus
  • Give buyers clear prices, photos and contact options
  • Keep the platform safe with reports and admin moderation
